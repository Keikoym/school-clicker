## Prompt final

Create a landscape 16:9 background image for a Brazilian school clicker game. First person view from the eyes of a seated student looking downward at their own school desk. A large empty pale warm wood desktop fills most of the frame, its far edge near the upper quarter of the image, its near edge at the bottom. The desktop must extend across the full width in the central and lower frame: empty usable surface for game sprites at x 12%-90%, y 30%-75%. Only a narrow softly simplified classroom background visible above the far edge. Clearly student viewpoint, not a product photograph showing desk legs, not a third person view, not a bird's eye overhead view. Attractive restrained hand-painted 2D game illustration with clean outlines and subtle wood grain, soft daylight, warm cream and muted green classroom tones, compatible with simple pixel-art school supplies placed on top later. All desktop completely empty: no books, no notebook, no stationery, no lamp, no people, no hands, no text, no numbers, no UI, no watermark. Wide composition, tranquil classroom atmosphere, low contrast surface so interactive objects will stand out. Produce just the background artwork.
